## DCMTK — DICOM data dictionary

The bundled DICOM data dictionary (`src/data/dictionary.json`, inlined into `dist/`)
is generated by `scripts/generateDictionary.ts` from DCMTK's `dcmdata/data/dicom.dic`,
which is vendored in this repository at `_configs/dicom.dic` with its original header
intact.

## DICOM standard

Tag definitions in the dictionary originate from the DICOM standard
(PS3.6 and PS3.7), published by NEMA. DICOM is a registered trademark of the
National Electrical Manufacturers Association.
